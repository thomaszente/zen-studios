ZEN STUDIOS

A temple for sound. A home for the music that moves people.

The vision

A studio where the gear is world-class and the room feels like a meditation.

ZEN STUDIOS is a production, recording and creative space in Austin — built for deep, emotive electronic music and the artists, healers and creators around it.

Why it needs to exist

Most studios are built for output. Few are built for flow.

Rooms without soul

Commercial studios are booked by the hour, lit like offices and designed around throughput — not the state artists create from.

Bedrooms without gear

Independent producers make records on laptops, missing the synths, monitoring and acoustics that finish a track properly.

Scenes without a home

Austin’s melodic and organic house community has dance floors, but no shared creative base to write, record and film together.

The space

Built like a temple. Tuned like a studio.

Control room — full-range monitoring, a curated wall of analog synths and drum machines, treated end to end.

Live room — percussion, guitars, keys and vocals, ready to capture organic textures.

Lounge & altar — candlelight, plants, stone and wood: a room you want to stay in.
